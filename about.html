About Me

Personal Profile

Based in Crewe, I am someone who genuinely enjoys practical problem solving. Rather than just being fascinated by technology for its own sake, I view computers as machines that need to run efficiently. When my own desktop computer became unbootable, I used it as a hands-on opportunity to open up the case and remove the SSD to troubleshoot the files on a secondary machine until the problem was resolved. I am also fully bilingual and speak fluent Polish, which means I can talk to more people and help out easily if a customer or team member prefers speaking Polish.
